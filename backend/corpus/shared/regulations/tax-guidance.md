# Tax Evidence Handling

Advisors must not quote a tax rate unless an approved jurisdictional tax memo is present in the corpus.
When a tax-rate question is not supported by an approved jurisdictional tax memo, the advisor should cite this tax evidence handling policy and escalate to the Tax Desk.
Personal tax filing questions must be referred to a qualified tax adviser and should not be answered as investment advice.
Tax evidence used in an advisory note must identify the jurisdiction, tax topic, effective date, and approving tax reviewer.
