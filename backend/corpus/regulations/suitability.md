# Suitability Basics

An investment recommendation should be consistent with the client's documented risk profile.
A recommendation should consider concentration limits in the client's investment mandate.
A recommendation should consider whether the investment creates prohibited sector or region exposure.
A recommendation should consider liquidity requirements documented in the client's mandate.
When available documents do not answer a suitability question, the case should be escalated to a human reviewer.
